# IMMERSION PRINCIPLES

The working rules that turn the philosophy into engineering decisions. Each is testable in review.

**1. The world renders first.** Every screen is a living scene with UI laid over it — never a solid app background with a picture inset. Even Settings and the Journal sit on a breathing landscape behind dark glass.

**2. Chrome dissolves.** Inside a biosphere, all interface fades to opacity 0.05 after 6.5 s of stillness and returns on touch. Nothing the user must watch may live in that layer.

**3. The day is for being; the night is for accounting.** No completion controls exist in daylight views. Habits appear as floating text. The only yes/no in the entire app lives inside the Grey Night. This asymmetry is the product.

**4. Motion has purpose or does not exist.** Approved motion vocabulary: environmental life (particles, glow drift, floats), threshold transitions (enterScene, crossfades, riseIn), and state breathing (the NOW dot). No motion for delight alone; nothing bounces.

**5. Entering is crossing a threshold.** Tapping a tile scales the world gently into place over one second. Leaving is a single quiet back action. Transitions are cinematic, not snappy.

**6. Sound is alive, sparse, and honest.** Each archetype has one sonic identity, synthesized live: broad beds with slow (≤ 0.1 Hz) movement plus rare events (a bird, a bell, a cricket). Crossfades, never cuts. Silence is scored deliberately — gaps between events are part of the composition. One chime (E5→A5) serves every completion and transition; no other UI sounds.

**7. Numbers whisper.** Streaks and counts render in the serif voice, small labels, muted dots. No number is ever red, animated on change, or accompanied by celebration.

**8. Copy speaks like dusk.** Short, warm, lowercase-leaning labels; serif italics for the human voice (*the day settles*, *rest well*); letterspaced caps only for wayfinding. Never exclamatory, never nagging, never "gamified" vocabulary (points, wins, crushing it).

**9. Nothing interrupts.** Toasts are the only unsolicited element: one at a time, bottom, self-dismissing, informational (*Forest begins*). No modals, no confirmation dialogs except the inline two-step reset, no badges, no notifications.

**10. Emptiness is welcomed, not scolded.** Empty states read as invitations (*This biosphere is open… simply be here*), never as failures to fix.

**11. Editing is backstage.** All dense controls live in Settings. The stage — home, immersion, night — offers at most: enter, leave, sound, answer.

**12. Performance is immersion.** A stuttering aurora breaks the spell faster than any design flaw. Frame budget wins over particle count; see NFR-1 and ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md budgets.
