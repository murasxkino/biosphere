3. WHAT IT DOES, WHO FOR, WHY
BIOSPHERE turns a day into a sequence of landscapes. The user divides the day into "biospheres" — named, timed stretches with a purpose, such as Tundra for morning deep focus — and each habit lives inside the one it belongs to. Each is a full-screen animated scene with its own soundscape; habits may carry a focus timer.
Nothing can be ticked off during the day. At an hour the user sets, the Grey Night falls and the app asks, one habit at a time, "Did you complete this today?", with room for a few words. The day is then sealed into a permanent journal. A Stillness view shows streaks and a 28-day rhythm.
Habit trackers optimise for streak pressure and notification volume, making people anxious about the tool rather than attentive to their lives. BIOSPHERE asks for honesty once a day instead of compliance all day. No accounts, social features, advertising, purchases or gamification.

4. SETUP AND ACCESS
No login, account, credentials or sample files are needed. Tap Enter on the onboarding screen; the app ships with example biospheres and habits.
IMPORTANT — the Grey Night (the reflection and sealing flow at the core of the app) is time-gated and will not appear during the day. To reach it: tap the sun icon (rightmost of four, top right) for Settings, scroll past the biospheres to the card "Grey Night — reflection begins", set its time a few minutes BEFORE the current time, then tap back. A Grey Night tile appears at the foot of the timeline. Tap it, then Begin. Answer each habit, optionally write a reflection, and seal the day. Sealed days appear in the Journal (document icon) and Stillness (seedling icon).
Sound begins on the first tap, never at launch — if silent, tap the speaker icon. Audio continues with the ring switch silenced and while locked. The notification prompt appears when a timer is first started, not at launch.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests of any kind and has no backend — no data providers, authentication, payments, AI or ML services, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or attribution SDKs, no cloud storage or sync. Soundtrack and typefaces are bundled; every landscape is drawn procedurally in code. All user data stays in private on-device storage. Built with React and Capacitor; the only runtime plugins are Local Notifications and Haptics, both entirely on-device.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None; the app functions identically everywhere and nothing is region-locked. Times and dates use the device's locale (17:00 versus 5:00 PM) — presentation only, not a difference in features or content. English only.
